Northwind Logistics Employee Handbook

This handbook is a test document written for evaluating the InsightDesk assistant. Northwind Logistics is a fictional company.

1. Welcome and Purpose

Welcome to Northwind Logistics. This handbook explains how we work, what we expect from each other, and where to find help. It is meant to be read once during your first week and used as a reference afterward. If something in this handbook is unclear, ask your direct manager or the people operations team. The handbook is reviewed once a year, and the latest version always replaces earlier ones.

2. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Sunday to Thursday, with a 45 minute unpaid lunch break. Teams may agree on flexible start times between 08:00 and 10:00 as long as the core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 are covered. Employees record their attendance in the time tracking tool at the start and end of each day. Missing entries must be corrected by the end of the following working day. Repeated unexplained absences are handled by the direct manager together with people operations.

3. IT Help Desk

The IT help desk is open Sunday to Thursday, from 08:00 to 18:00. Requests are submitted through the internal ticket portal, and each ticket receives an automatic confirmation number. Urgent issues that block your work should be marked as high priority. The help desk handles laptop problems, account access, software requests, and printer issues. It does not handle personal devices or home internet problems. Typical response time for normal tickets is one working day.

4. Accounts and Passwords

Every employee receives a personal company account. Accounts are protected by a password of at least 14 characters and by multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be changed every 120 days. Sharing passwords between colleagues is not allowed under any circumstances. If you suspect your account was used by someone else, change your password immediately and open a high priority ticket. Shared mailboxes use delegated access instead of shared passwords.

5. Equipment and Laptops

New employees receive a company laptop, a docking station, and a headset. Laptops use full disk encryption, and this setting must never be disabled. Equipment remains the property of the company and must be returned on the last working day. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the help desk within 24 hours. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of their equipment, including keeping it away from food and drinks.

6. Software Installation

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed on company laptops. If you need a tool that is not in the catalog, submit a software request with a short business reason. The security team reviews each request and usually answers within five working days. Browser extensions count as software and follow the same process. Pirated or cracked software is strictly forbidden and may lead to disciplinary action.

7. Email Etiquette

Use clear subject lines and keep messages short. Reply to internal emails within two working days, even if only to say that you need more time. Do not use reply all unless everyone on the thread needs your answer. Avoid sending large attachments, and share links to the document storage instead. Messages that contain customer data must be sent only to approved recipients.

8. Meetings

Meetings should have an agenda and a clear owner. Invitations are sent at least one day in advance, except for urgent matters. Meetings start and end on time, and notes with action items are shared within one working day. Recurring meetings are reviewed every quarter, and those that no longer provide value are cancelled. Camera use in video meetings is encouraged but not required.

9. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be approved by the direct manager before booking. Flights and hotels are booked through the approved travel agency. Employees submit expense reports with receipts within 30 days of the expense. Meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit that is published on the finance page. Personal expenses, such as entertainment or minibar purchases, are not reimbursed. Questions about expenses go to the finance team.

10. Leave and Holidays

Full time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per year, in addition to public holidays. Leave requests are submitted in the people system and approved by the direct manager. Up to five unused days may be carried over to the next year. Leave of more than ten consecutive working days should be planned at least a month in advance. Teams are expected to arrange coverage before leaving.

11. Sick Leave

Employees who are unwell should notify their direct manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than two consecutive days. Sick leave is paid according to local law. Employees should not work while ill unless they choose to and feel able. Information about your health is confidential and is shared only with people operations when necessary.

12. Remote Work

Northwind supports remote work for roles where it is practical. Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. Requests for remote work days must be submitted at least five working days in advance and approved by the direct manager. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours and must work from a private location. Company laptops are the only devices allowed for work with customer data. Working from another country is not permitted without written approval from people operations.

13. Office Access and Badges

Each employee receives a personal access badge on the first day. Badges must be worn visibly inside the office and must never be lent to another person. A lost badge should be reported to the facilities desk right away so that it can be blocked. Replacement badges are issued within one working day. Access to the server room and the finance area is limited to named employees and reviewed every six months.

14. Visitors

All visitors must be registered at the reception desk before entering the office. Visitors receive a temporary badge and must be accompanied by their host at all times. Hosts are responsible for the behavior of their visitors. Visitors may use the guest network but never the internal network. Photography inside the office is not allowed without permission. Deliveries are accepted only at the reception desk.

15. Data Classification

Company information is divided into four levels: public, internal, confidential, and restricted. Public information may be shared freely. Internal information stays within the company. Confidential information, such as customer contracts, is shared only with those who need it for their work. Restricted information, such as employee salaries and security reports, requires explicit approval for every access. When in doubt, treat the information as one level higher than you think.

16. Customer Communication

Customers are our most important partners, and every message to them reflects on the company. Respond to customer requests within one working day. Be honest about delays and problems, and propose a next step. Do not promise delivery dates that you cannot confirm with the operations team. Complaints are logged in the customer system and followed up by the service manager. Never share information about one customer with another.

17. Procurement

Purchases above a defined amount require at least two price quotes and approval from the department head. Contracts with suppliers are reviewed by the legal team before signing. Employees may not sign contracts on behalf of the company unless they have written signing authority. Gifts from suppliers above a symbolic value must be declined or reported to the compliance officer. Supplier performance is reviewed once a year.

18. Training and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget that can be used for courses, conferences, and books. Training requests are agreed with the direct manager. New employees complete mandatory courses on security awareness and workplace conduct during the first month. Employees who attend external training are encouraged to share what they learned with their team. Internal mentoring is available and can be requested through people operations.

19. Performance Reviews

Performance is discussed twice a year, in a mid year check in and a full annual review. Employees write a short self assessment before each review. Managers give specific examples of strengths and areas to improve. Goals for the next period are agreed together and written down. Pay changes and promotions are decided after the annual review cycle and are not discussed during the mid year check in.

20. Health and Safety

Everyone is responsible for a safe workplace. Report hazards such as wet floors, damaged cables, or blocked exits to the facilities desk. The office has first aid kits on every floor, and a list of trained first aiders is posted next to each kit. Workstations should be set up ergonomically, and the facilities team can adjust chairs and desks on request. Employees are encouraged to take short breaks from screens during the day.

21. Emergency Procedures

In case of a fire alarm, stop working, leave the building by the nearest exit, and do not use the elevators. Do not return to the building until the building warden says that it is safe. Floor wardens check their floors and report to the building warden. In case of a medical emergency, call the national emergency number first and then notify the facilities desk. Evacuation drills are held once a year, and participation is mandatory.

22. Facilities Maintenance

The facilities desk is open Sunday to Thursday, from 07:00 to 16:00, and handles heating, lighting, cleaning, and repairs. Office cleaning takes place every evening after working hours. Kitchen supplies are refilled twice a week. Maintenance requests are submitted through the same ticket portal as IT requests, using the facilities category. Planned maintenance that may affect work, such as water or power shutdowns, is announced at least three working days in advance.

The backup power generator is tested every second Tuesday of the month at 06:30, before the office opens. During the test the lights may flicker briefly, and no action is required from employees.

23. Closing

Thank you for reading this handbook. Our workplace works best when everyone takes responsibility, asks questions early, and helps colleagues. If you have suggestions for improving this handbook, send them to people operations. We wish you a successful and enjoyable time at Northwind Logistics.
